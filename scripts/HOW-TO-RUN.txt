EndFare Tour & Travel's API Demo
================================

No installation needed.

HOW TO RUN
----------
1. Double-click EndFareDemo.exe
2. The demo opens automatically in Google Chrome (or your default browser
   if Chrome isn't installed). If it doesn't, open http://localhost:3000.
3. Keep the black console window open while using the demo.
   Close it to stop the demo.

FIRST-TIME SETUP
----------------
1. Click the gear icon (Settings) at the top-right.
2. Enter your Client ID and Client Secret, then click "Save Credentials".
3. Optional: enter your company name and upload your logo, then click "Save Branding".
4. Go to Flights or Hotels and try a search.

YOUR DATA
---------
Your credentials and branding are saved in the "config" folder next to
EndFareDemo.exe on your computer. They are never sent anywhere except to the
EndFare API when you run a search.

To move the demo to another computer, copy the whole folder including "config".

WINDOWS SECURITY WARNING
------------------------
If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", click "More info" then "Run anyway".
If Windows Firewall asks for access, you can click "Cancel" - the demo only runs
on your own computer.
